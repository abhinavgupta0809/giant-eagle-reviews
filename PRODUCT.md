# Product

Written Oct 20, 2026 for the Giant Eagle run.

## Platform

web

## Users

- Abhinav and his project team, working on a client brief: how Giant Eagle can build engagement with Gen Z.
- The Giant Eagle client team, if Abhinav shares it. They know the app and myPerks far better than the reviews do, so the site leads with what reviewers say and the check to run next, not with what to build.

## Purpose

Show what a year of written app reviews says about the Giant Eagle app, why that differs from the 4.5-star store rating, how it maps to the areas in the client's brief, and how it sits next to Kroger's SEC filings on the pending acquisition.

## Constraints

- No typed figures: every number on the site is computed from `site/data/*.json`.
- Store reviews carry no age; nothing here measures Gen Z directly, and the site says so.
- Independent and unofficial: no logo or wordmark, footer disclaimer, Giant Eagle's colors only.
