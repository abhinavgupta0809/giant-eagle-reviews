# Design

The layout, components, and chart rules carry over from earlier runs in this series (Perplexity through Snapchat); see any of those repos' `docs/DESIGN.md` for the full system.

What is specific to this run:

- **Palette** from gianteagle.com's stylesheets on 2026-10-20: red `#C8102E` (masthead, accents), slate `#2C3337` (text, footer), warm neutrals `#F9F8F6` / `#F0EEEB` / `#E5E5E3`, green `#1F7A40` (praise), blue `#0072CE`. Derived shades are listed in the token comment at the top of `site/styles.css`.
- **Complaints are orange `#C84700`, not red**, so Giant Eagle's red never reads as "bad".
- **Fonts:** Outfit for headings (the site's main face), Inter for body.
- **Small-corpus settings:** categories under 10 reviews are faded (30 in earlier runs), the trend chart is monthly, Android versions show from 8 reviews.
- **Added sections:** "Why Google Play shows 4.5 stars" on the Overview, "Reviews by area of the brief" in Context, and a Filings tab built on Kroger's SEC filings.
